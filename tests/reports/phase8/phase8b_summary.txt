ANSWER:
The admission routes for M.Sc. programs at IIT Jodhpur are as follows: 1. **Joint Admission Test for Master's (JAM)**: Applicants qualified through the JAM organized by IITs are eligible for admission. 2. **Institute-Conducted Written Test/Interview**: Applicants with a bachelor’s degree in a relevant discipline (3- or 4-year program) may be admitted based on a written test and/or personal interview conducted by IIT Jodhpur. **Eligibility Criteria**: - Applicants must have a valid GATE score, exemption from GATE as per MHRD rules, or qualify in an equivalent national examination. - Alternatively, they may qualify through IIT Jodhpur’s own written test and/or interview. - A minimum of 70% marks (or 7.0 CPI/CGPA) in the qualifying degree is required for GEN/GEN-EWS/OBC categories, and 65% for SC/ST/PD candidates. These routes are detailed in the academic regulations for M.Sc. admissions.
