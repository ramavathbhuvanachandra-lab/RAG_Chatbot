# MBA Admission and Selection Process

## Scope

Authoritative admission criteria, eligibility norms, and shortlisting methodology for the Master of Business Administration (Tech MBA) programme at the School of Management and Entrepreneurship (SME), IIT Jodhpur.

## Two-Stage Selection Process

Admission to the MBA programme consists of two sequential phases:

### Phase A: Initial Shortlisting

Candidates are shortlisted based on a composite score evaluated across the following parameters:
* Common Admission Test (CAT) Overall Scaled Score
* Full-Time Work Experience Score
* Gender Diversity Score

#### Cutoffs and Work Experience Guidelines:
* Cutoffs: Sectional and overall CAT percentile cutoffs are determined dynamically based on application pool size.
* Valid Experience: Work experience must be post-qualifying degree in registered corporate entities or government bodies.
* Exclusions: Experience gained in coaching centres, self-proprietary retail, unorganized retail, NGOs, or petrol pumps is not eligible.
* Documentation: Experience letters, joining letters, and first/last month salary slips must be produced during personal interviews. Unpaid work, internships, and article-ships are excluded.

### Phase B: Final Shortlisting and Selection

Shortlisted candidates participate in Personal Interviews conducted via virtual mode. The final merit ranking is computed from:
* Composite Score Weightage
* Personal Interview Performance
* Relevant Academic Performance and Category Relaxations under Government of India norms.
