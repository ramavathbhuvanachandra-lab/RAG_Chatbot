# Admission of Non-Degree and Casual Students

## Scope

Authoritative institutional regulations governing non-degree students, visiting research scholars, and casual course registration at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## 20.3 Admission of Non-Degree Students

A non-degree student is defined as an academic scholar who is registered for a degree in a recognized institute or university in India or abroad, and who is officially sponsored by that institute or university to complete part of his or her academic requirements at IIT Jodhpur.

Key operational guidelines for non-degree students:
* Academic Scope: Non-degree students may carry out specialised research, take approved courses for credit or audit, or utilise academic, computing, and laboratory infrastructure.
* Casual Course Admission: Any candidate satisfying the minimum eligibility criteria specified in Section 20.2 can be admitted as a casual student for specific courses.
* Routing and Approval: All applications for non-degree and casual admission must be officially routed through and approved by the Office of Dean (Research and Development).
* Institutional Governance: Students admitted under this category are strictly governed by all rules, academic regulations, safety guidelines, and disciplinary codes of IIT Jodhpur.
