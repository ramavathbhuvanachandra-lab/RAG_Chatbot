# Ph.D. Admissions and Regulations

## Scope

Authoritative eligibility criteria, admission categories, and selection requirements for Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) programmes across all academic units at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## 20.2.6 Admission to Ph.D. Programme (Regular Full-Time)

Candidates admitted under the Regular category must dedicate full-time study and cannot be full-time or part-time employees of any other organization:
* Master's Degree Route: Master’s degree in engineering, pharmacy, agricultural science, science, humanities, social sciences, or management with at least 60% marks or 6.0/10 CGPA for GEN/GEN-EWS/OBC (55% or 5.5/10 for SC/ST/PwD).
* Professional Degree Route: MBBS with MD/MS, BVSc with MVSc, BDS with MDS with at least 60% marks or 6.0/10 CGPA.
* Four-Year Bachelor's Route: Four-year bachelor’s degree in engineering, science, or technology with at least 70% marks or 7.0/10 CGPA for GEN/GEN-EWS/OBC (65% or 6.5/10 for SC/ST/PwD).
* National Examination: Valid GATE score, UGC-NET (JRF), CSIR-NET (JRF), NBHM, or equivalent national fellowship.

## 20.2.9 Admission to Ph.D. (Sponsored, External, and Part-Time)

* Academic Qualifications: Same qualifying degrees and academic grade thresholds as specified for Regular Ph.D. candidates.
* Work Experience: Minimum of two years of active professional work experience in industry, R&D organizations, or accredited academic institutions.
* Selection Process: Performance in departmental written examination and technical interview.

## Rolling PhD Advertisements and Interdisciplinary Initiatives

IIT Jodhpur maintains rolling advertisements for Ph.D. admissions in frontier and interdisciplinary fields across departments, schools, and research platforms.
