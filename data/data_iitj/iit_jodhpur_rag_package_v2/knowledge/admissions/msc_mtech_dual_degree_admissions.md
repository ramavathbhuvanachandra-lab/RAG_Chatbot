# Admission to M.Sc. – M.Tech. Dual Degree Programme

## Scope

Authoritative admission criteria and eligibility guidelines for the M.Sc. – M.Tech. Dual Degree Programme at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## 20.2.2 Admission Avenues (Regular)

Admission to the M.Sc. – M.Tech. Dual Degree Programmes offered by IIT Jodhpur is conducted through two distinct avenues:
* Joint Admission Test for Masters (JAM): National rank-based centralized merit list organized by the Indian Institutes of Technology.
* Institutional Entrance: Applicants holding a bachelor's degree in any relevant discipline (3- or 4-year programme) admitted based on written test and/or personal interview conducted by IIT Jodhpur.

## Academic Eligibility Criteria

Candidates applying under institutional written test/interview must satisfy:
* Aggregate Marks: Minimum 60% marks for GEN/GEN-EWS/OBC category (55% for SC/ST/PwD) in aggregate or equivalent first-class designation.
* Grade Point Threshold: Minimum CGPA of 6.0 on a 10-point scale for GEN/GEN-EWS/OBC (5.5 for SC/ST/PwD), with proportional scaling on other grading scales (such as 4.8 on an 8-point scale).
