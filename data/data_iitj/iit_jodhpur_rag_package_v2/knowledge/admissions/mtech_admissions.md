# M.Tech Admissions and Regulations

## Scope

Authoritative admission regulations, eligibility conditions, and category requirements for Master of Technology (M.Tech.) programmes at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## 20.2.3 Admission to M.Tech. Programme (Regular)

* Qualifying Degree: Bachelor’s degree in engineering or science (4-year programme) or a master’s degree in science, MCA, Pharmacy, Medical Sciences, Agricultural Sciences, or Veterinary Sciences in relevant disciplines.
* Minimum Academic Score: Minimum 60% marks for GEN/GEN-EWS/OBC (55% for SC/ST/PwD) in aggregate, or a minimum CGPA of 6.0 on a 10-point scale (5.5 for SC/ST/PwD).
* Entrance Qualification: Valid GATE score in the relevant discipline, or exemption as per Ministry of Education norms for CFTI graduates with high CGPA, or qualification in departmental written test/interview.

## 20.2.4 Admission to M.Tech. Programme (Executive, Part-Time, External, and Sponsored)

* Qualifying Degree: Bachelor’s degree in engineering or science (4-year programme) or master’s degree in science, MCA, Pharmacy, or related fields.
* Minimum Academic Score: Minimum 60% marks for GEN/GEN-EWS/OBC (55% for SC/ST/PwD) in aggregate or CGPA of 6.0 on a 10-point scale.
* Professional Work Experience: Minimum two years of professional work experience in industry, R&D laboratories, or academic institutions at the time of registration.
* Selection Process: Qualified performance in written test and/or personal interview conducted by IIT Jodhpur.
