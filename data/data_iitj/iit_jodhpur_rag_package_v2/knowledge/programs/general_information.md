Scholarship of Teaching and Learning for Innovative Pedagogies: What it is, Why and How to do it
The Scholarship of Teaching and Learning (SoTL) is not simply teaching well, it is about approaching your teaching as if it were a research topic and finding new ways of teaching using innovative pedagogies. For example, in the context of eLearning, just providing traditional teaching and learning content through eLearning portals is gross underutilisation of the potential of current technologies. We should explore how we can enhance student engagement, and learning outcomes through innovative pedagogies such as:
* Digital Storytelling
* Game-based Learning
* Drama-based Learning
Seminar: The seminar will explore how can we research and incorporate such innovations in our teaching.

## Our Programs


TISC offers three flexible incubation pathways designed to support startups at different stages of development. Each program is tailored to your needs, from ideation through scaling.


## Physical Incubation Programme


**For:** Registered startups (DPIIT-recognized) ready to build and scale
**Duration:** 2–3 years (extendable up to 5 years for Deep Tech startups)
**Facilitation Charges:** ₹2,000 + 18% GST per month (co-working space)
**Support Includes:**
* Full infrastructure access (co-working spaces, laboratories, meeting facilities)
* Seed funding connections and funding assistance
* Comprehensive mentorship from faculty and industry experts
* Domestic and international market access opportunities
* PR support, human resource sourcing, and team building assistance
* Fellowship programs and grant linkages
* Community engagement and networking events
**Best For:** Startups with registered entities, technical products, and plans to scale within India and globally.


## Program Objectives


To ensure that future educators are equipped with the skills, especially in the area of science, technology, and pedagogy, which are required to adapt to evolving educational needs, the objectives of the 4-year Integrated BSc-BEd Program (ITEP) for the Secondary Stage at IIT Jodhpur include:
1. Developing necessary competencies in graduates for teaching at the secondary stage of school education (classes 9 to 12) (as envisioned by NEP and NCTE Curriculum Framework, p.18)
2. Capacity building in the area of Science & Technology Education in India
3. Developing pedagogical skills among teachers in India
4. Enabling technology integration in the classroom for future learners of India
5. Building connection with the society and inculcate the required communication skills



1. Developing a comprehensive understanding of the learning process and how to design, deliver and enrich instruction that meets the needs of diverse learners
2. Enabling educators to adopt innovative teaching and assessment strategies that leverage technology and hands-on experience with edtech tools and resources
3. Providing educators an exposure to state-of-the-art and emerging trends in education

## Curriculum


The curriculum of the ITEP program at IIT Jodhpur is based on the NCTE guidelines. In addition to the guidelines received, IIT Jodhpur has curated a Curriculum for the BSc-BEd programme that is research-based, drawing on the state-of-the-art research in education as well as education technology, lessons from innovative and contemporary best practices in teacher education and pedagogical practices inspired by the rich philosophical and cultural traditions of our country.
The pedagogy of the BSc-BEd program will seek to build a strong foundation for learning, with a focus on (i) strong disciplinary knowledge, (ii) technology integration, (iii) learning opportunities for making connections between the local, national and global levels, (iv) exemplary commitment to equality, equity, inclusivity and social justice in every interaction with students, and (v) upholding the best of our nation’s values and cultural traditions, for this present period.
**The USP of the Jodhpur model** in terms of curriculum enhancement and pedagogical innovation will have the following aspects:
1. **The design and content of the Science Courses** are designed keeping in mind the multiple exit options in this program, as well as the need to prepare interested students to pursue further studies in their chosen Science Major.
2. **A focus on Education Technology** spread over the last four semesters of the program, so that students will be able to apply the theoretical insights they have gained.
3. **A model for local engagement** which will demonstrate how the knowledge, practices and traditions of local communities can be used by the students in their future development of lesson plans and content which engage children, etc.
4. **Diversity of school exposure experiences** will be provided to the students in addition to the school practicum component, beginning from their first year.
