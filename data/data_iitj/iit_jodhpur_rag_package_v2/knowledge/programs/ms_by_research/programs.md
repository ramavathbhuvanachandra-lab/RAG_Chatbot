# M.S. by Research — Programs

## Scope

Authoritative institutional knowledge for M.S. by Research — Programs at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Program Features


* Opportunities for collaborative projects across disciplines.
* Practice-based outputs, including new art forms and entrepreneurial ventures.
* Focus on inclusivity and livelihood generation for disenfranchised artisans.
* Flexible coursework and simultaneous research options tailored to student interests.


## Program Structure


1.) Eligibility: Bachelor's (B.Tech. or equivalent, 4 years) or Master's (M.A./M.Sc., 5 years) with a strong academic record.
2.) Duration:
* Full-time: 2–4 years.
* Part-time: 3–5 years.
3.) Credits:
* Coursework: 24 credits.
* Thesis: 36 credits.
4.) Course Flexibility: Students customize coursework from advanced courses across disciplines, guided by the ADI Research Committee.
