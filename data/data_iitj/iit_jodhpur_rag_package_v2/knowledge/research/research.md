# Research Platforms and Focus Areas

## Scope

Authoritative institutional overview of research platforms, transdisciplinary education technology, and creative arts research at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Transdisciplinary Research Areas

Key frontier research domains supported across academic platforms:
* [Blended Learning and Flipped Classroom](https://www.iitj.ac.in/cete/en/blended-learning-and-flipped-classroom)
* [Game-based Learning for Eng. Education](https://www.iitj.ac.in/cete/en/game-based-learning-for-engineering-education)
* [Immersive Education](https://www.iitj.ac.in/cete/en/immersive-education)
* [Behavioral Studies](https://www.iitj.ac.in/cete/en/behavioral-studies)
* [Education Policy](https://www.iitj.ac.in/cete/en/education-policy)

The future of higher education requires moving from test-driven grade-centric fixed curriculum frameworks into curiosity-driven, learning-centric personalized paradigms. Education should be flexible, individualized, and designed to integrate technical disciplines with real-world social contexts.

## Creative Arts and Digital Immersion

The Centre of Excellence for Arts and Digital Immersion (ADI) explores innovative intersections between artificial intelligence, creative technologies, and cultural heritage:
* AI and Creative Arts: Utilizing AI for generative artwork, musical composition, digital storytelling, and exploring ethical questions around digital authorship.
* Mixed-Media and Immersive Art: Redefining visual arts through immersive Virtual Reality (VR), Augmented Reality (AR), and Mixed Reality (MR) installations.
