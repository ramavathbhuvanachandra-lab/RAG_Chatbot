# Hostel Accommodation — General Information

## Scope

Authoritative institutional knowledge for Hostel Accommodation — General Information at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Residential Campus Model

Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur operates a fully residential campus that forms an integral part of academic life, fostering collaboration and learning beyond classrooms.

IIT Jodhpur features 17 student hostels, comprising 5 girls' hostels and 12 boys' hostels situated within the Institute campus. Accommodation allocation is governed by Institute policies based on academic program (UG/PG/PhD), year of residence, and availability. All first-year students (UG, PG, and PhD) are accommodated in rooms on a double-occupancy basis.

## Hostel Booking and Room Allotment

Hostel accommodation is managed by the Office of Students (Hostel Affairs) through an online ERP-based booking and allotment system. Short-term accommodation is available for visitors, interns, executive students, and participants of institute immersion programs for stays not exceeding 10 days.

## Dining and Mess Facilities

Separate dining facilities (vegetarian and non-vegetarian messes) operate on campus. Daily meals (breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, dinner) are served on a subscription basis, with payments made directly to authorized mess vendors.

## Anti-Ragging Framework

IIT Jodhpur enforces a strict zero-tolerance anti-ragging policy overseen by the Anti-Ragging Committee (chaired by the Dean of Student Affairs) and the Anti-Ragging Squad (chaired by the Associate Dean of Hostel Affairs, with wardens of all 17 hostels).
