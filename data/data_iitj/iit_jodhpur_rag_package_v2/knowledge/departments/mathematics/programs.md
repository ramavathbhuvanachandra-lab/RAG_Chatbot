# Mathematics — Departmental Programmes Directory

## Scope

Comprehensive index and navigational directory of undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral, and minor academic programmes offered by the Department of Mathematics at IIT Jodhpur.

## Academic Offerings Directory

The Department of Mathematics delivers specialized programmes spanning foundational mathematical theory and advanced computational science:

* Undergraduate Programmes:
- B.S. in Mathematics and Computing: Four-year degree covering analysis, algebra, probability, machine learning, and algorithmic finance. Refer to [B.S. in Mathematics and Computing](bs_mathematics_and_computing.md).
- Minor in Mathematics and Computing: Seven-course, 19-credit specialization for non-major engineering undergraduates. Refer to [Minor in Mathematics and Computing](minor_mathematics_and_computing.md).
* Postgraduate Programmes:
- M.Sc. in Mathematics: Two-year degree admitted through IIT JAM. Refer to [M.Sc. in Mathematics](msc_mathematics.md).
- M.Sc. – M.Tech. Dual Degree in Mathematics and Data Science.
- M.Tech. in Data and Computational Sciences.
* Doctoral Research:
- Ph.D. in Mathematics: Theoretical and computational doctoral research across functional analysis, numerical methods, differential equations, fluid dynamics, and cryptography.
