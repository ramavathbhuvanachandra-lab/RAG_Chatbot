# M.Sc. in Mathematics and Postgraduate Programmes

## Scope

Authoritative specifications, objectives, and academic avenues for Master of Science (M.Sc.) and related postgraduate programmes in the Department of Mathematics at IIT Jodhpur.

## Programme Objectives

The M.Sc. in Mathematics programme is designed to nurture motivated scholars in frontier areas of pure and applied mathematics. It aims to develop a specialized workforce capable of solving mathematical challenges in scientific, industrial, and financial organizations while providing rigorous preparation for doctoral research.

## Postgraduate Offerings in Mathematics

The Department of Mathematics hosts the following advanced degree programmes:
* M.Sc. in Mathematics: Two-year regular programme admitted through the Joint Admission Test for Masters (IIT JAM).
* M.Sc. – M.Tech. Dual Degree in Mathematics and Data Science: Integrated four-year postgraduate programme combining theoretical mathematics with practical data science architectures.
* M.Tech. in Data and Computational Sciences: Advanced engineering programme focusing on high-performance computing, numerical analysis, and machine learning.
* M.Tech. – Ph.D. Dual Degree in Data and Computational Sciences: Seamless pathway from postgraduate specialization into doctoral dissertation research.
