## Energy, Environment, and Global Economy


Research in this area addresses the interconnected challenges of climate change, energy transitions, sustainability, agricultural transformation, and the evolving global economic order. The department examines how environmental change, trade systems, and resource transitions shape development pathways, livelihoods, and economic resilience.
**Current research themes include the following:**
* Economics of climate-smart technologies - Climate adaptation, sustainability transitions, green technologies, and environmental policy
* Trade and geo-strategy - International trade, global value chains, geopolitical economic interactions, and strategic economic systems
* Resource and energy transitions - Energy economics, green transitions, renewable systems, and low-carbon development pathways
* Agricultural and infrastructure economics - Agricultural transformation, climate-resilient systems, infrastructure development, and rural transitions
* Urban and regional sustainability - Urban economics, migration, resource allocation, and environmental governance

## Digital Economy and Computational Economics


Research in this area examines the transformation of economies through digital technologies, computational methods, financial innovation, and data-driven systems. The department focuses on understanding digital markets, platform-based business models, fintech ecosystems, monetary dynamics, and emerging analytical tools for economic modelling and forecasting.
**Current research themes include the following:**
* Digital and platform economics - Market design, digital ecosystems, platform governance, and technology-enabled service systems
* Fintech and blockchain - Monetary systems, financial intermediation, inflation dynamics, green finance, and financial innovation
* Big data and computational economics - Computational modelling, policy analytics, applied econometrics, forecasting, and time-series analysis
* Applied game theory and operations research - Strategic interactions, optimisation, service operations, and analytics-driven decision-making
* Macroeconomic interactions and financial systems - Firm dynamics, macro-finance, fiscal and monetary policy, and global economic linkages
