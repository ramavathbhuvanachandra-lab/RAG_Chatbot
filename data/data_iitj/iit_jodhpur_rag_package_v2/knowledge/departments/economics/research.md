## Research Areas


Find out about our research areas, thematic expertise, and faculty research interests in the Department of Economics at IIT Jodhpur.
The Department of Economics at IIT Jodhpur promotes interdisciplinary and technology-driven research to address emerging economic, environmental, and societal challenges. The department integrates economics with data science, engineering systems, sustainability studies, and policy analytics to develop actionable and scalable solutions for national development.
