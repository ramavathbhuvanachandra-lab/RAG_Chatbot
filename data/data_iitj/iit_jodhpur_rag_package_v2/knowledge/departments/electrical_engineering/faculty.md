# Electrical Engineering — Faculty

## Scope

Authoritative institutional knowledge for Electrical Engineering — Faculty at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Department Faculty Directory

The Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Jodhpur comprises faculty members actively engaged in teaching and transdisciplinary research across communications, signal processing, VLSI design, control systems, power engineering, and smart sensors.
