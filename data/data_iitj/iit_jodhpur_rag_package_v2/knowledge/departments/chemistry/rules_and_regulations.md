# Chemistry — Rules and Regulations

## Scope

Authoritative institutional knowledge for Chemistry — Rules and Regulations at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## 7. Refund Policy


In case of excess payment, no monetary refund will be processed. Instead, additional sample analyses will be carried out to adjust the excess amount.
######


## 2. Payment Guidelines


* Payments must be made only through the ERP portal.
* Direct transfers to any bank account, DDs, cheques, or cash payments are not accepted.
* Only electronic payments initiated via the ERP system will be considered valid.
* After successful payment and receipt of samples, slots will be allotted for analysis.
######


## 3. Sample Submission Policy


* The Department receives a large number of samples from across India; hence, prior coordination is essential.
* Do not dispatch samples without prior discussion with the respective lab in-charge.
* Samples received without prior intimation or confirmation will be processed only when slots are available.
