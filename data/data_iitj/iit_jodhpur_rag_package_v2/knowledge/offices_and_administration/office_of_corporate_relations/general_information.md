## Scholarships & Student Support


STEM for HER Scholarship Program
Twelve female students from IIT Jodhpur have been selected for the prestigious **STEM for HER Scholarship Program** , a CSR initiative by Genpact in collaboration with United Way Hyderabad.
**Program Benefits:**
* Structured mentorship from industry professionals throughout the academic year
This initiative empowers young women in technology by providing financial support and professional guidance. Students were selected through a rigorous evaluation process, emerging as top achievers from across the country.
Udaan Scholarship
Three students from IIT Jodhpur have been selected as recipients of the prestigious **Udaan Scholarship** , supported by TransUnion CIBIL and facilitated through FORSTU.
**Awardees:**
* Mr. Mota Ram
* Mr. Prince Kumar Jain
* Mr. Kachhawa Goutham
