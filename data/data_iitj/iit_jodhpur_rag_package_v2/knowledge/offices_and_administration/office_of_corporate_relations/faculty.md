## Endowments & Chair Professorships


Endowed Genpact Chair Professorship
IIT Jodhpur is proud to announce the creation of the endowment for establishment of the **Endowed Genpact Chair Professorship** in the field of Computer Science, Data Science, and AI.
**Focus Area:** Cutting-edge research and education in AI, Data Science, and related domains
This strategic partnership marks a significant step in fostering advanced research, talent development, and industry-academia collaboration at IIT Jodhpur.
iHub Drishti Chanakya Chair Professorship
In a major boost to frontier research at IIT Jodhpur, **iHub Drishti Foundation** has endowed the Institute with ₹1.66 Crores to establish two prestigious Chair Professorships.
**Objective:** To advance research, teaching, and innovation in immersive and intelligent visual technologies
This collaboration strengthens IIT Jodhpur's role as a leading research hub in next-generation computing and XR technologies.
IVAAN Foundation - Clean Energy Endowments
**IVAAN Foundation,** under its initiative of empowering individuals and fostering innovation has endowed the Institute with ₹1.75 Crores.
**Establishes:**
* Endowed Goliya Chair Professorship in Clean Energy
* Endowed Johari Young Faculty Fellowship

**Objective:** Focus on driving impactful education, cutting-edge research, and societal contributions in the domain of Clean Energy at the Rishabh Center for Research and Innovation at IIT Jodhpur.
