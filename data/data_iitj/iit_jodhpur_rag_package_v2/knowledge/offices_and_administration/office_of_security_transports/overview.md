# Office of Security & Transports — Overview

## Scope

Authoritative institutional knowledge for Office of Security & Transports — Overview at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Mandate and Operations

The Office of Security & Transports ensures campus safety and manages comprehensive transportation services for students, faculty, staff, and visitors.

### Campus Security

- 24/7 security patrols, surveillance systems, and incident response.
- Close coordination with local administrative authorities and emergency service teams.
- Conduction of regular campus safety drills and awareness programs.

### Transportation & Mobility

- Operation of campus shuttles and transport logistics for academic events, field trips, and daily commutes.
