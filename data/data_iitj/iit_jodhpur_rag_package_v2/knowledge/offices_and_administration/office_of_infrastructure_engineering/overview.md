# Office of Infrastructure Engineering (OIE) — Overview

## Scope

Authoritative institutional knowledge for Office of Infrastructure Engineering (OIE) — Overview at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Department Responsibilities

The Office of Infrastructure Engineering (OIE) is the engineering works unit of IIT Jodhpur. It is responsible for the planning, design, and construction of new infrastructure facilities across the 852-acre campus, as well as managing operation, maintenance, upgrades, and electrical/civil services.
