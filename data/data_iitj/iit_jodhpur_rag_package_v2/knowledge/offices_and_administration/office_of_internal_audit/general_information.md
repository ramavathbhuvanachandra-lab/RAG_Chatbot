# Office of Internal Audit — General Information

## Scope

Authoritative institutional knowledge for Office of Internal Audit — General Information at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Objectives of Internal Audit


While external audits mostly focus on financial risk, internal audit has a wider range of responsibilities and different reporting requirements. The objectives of Internal Audit are as follows:-
* To help in risk management
* To comply with all relevant laws and regulations of the government/organizations
* To identify weaknesses in an organization’s internal procedures and suggest improvements.


## Responsibilities of Internal Audit


While external audits mostly focus on financial risk, internal audit has a wider range of responsibilities and different reporting requirements. The objectives of Internal Audit are as follows:-
* Oversee the implementation of policies, procedures, orders, and other guidelines of the Institute.
* Pursue with statutory audit office to drop/settle objections /audit paras raised by them during an audit of accounts of the Institute.
* Examine and report to management on audit observations/irregularities brought to the notice by the statutory audit.
* Examine and ensure the financial activities of the Institute are carried out as per the prevailing laws/norms of the Institute.
* Evaluate financial and operational procedures to assure adequate internal controls are present.
* Identify, assess, and evaluate the Institute’s risk areas; make appropriate recommendations for improved internal controls and accounting procedures.
