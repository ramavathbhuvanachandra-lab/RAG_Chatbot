# Digital Infrastructure & Automation (DIA) / Computer Center

## Scope

Authoritative institutional knowledge for Digital Infrastructure & Automation (DIA) / Computer Center at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Role and Facilities

The Digital Infrastructure & Automation (DIA) division, commonly known as the Computer Center at IIT Jodhpur, serves as the central hub for computing, network engineering, and information technology. It provides core computing infrastructure, high-speed campus networking, and enterprise automation services to support research and academic operations.

Equipped with state-of-the-art server infrastructure, centralized storage, and specialized technical personnel, DIA fosters an advanced computing environment for the entire campus community.
