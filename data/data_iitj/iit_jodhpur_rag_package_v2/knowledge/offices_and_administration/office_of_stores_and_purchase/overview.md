# Office of Stores and Purchase — Overview

## Scope

Authoritative institutional knowledge for Office of Stores and Purchase — Overview at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Welcome To Office of Stores & Purchase


| The Office of Stores & Purchase facilitates the following: |
| --- |
| 1. | Purchase of equipment & stores; |
| 2. | Maintenance of Asset Register; Stock Verification; |
| 3. | Maintenance of Control Store; Maintenance of lists of stockists and dealers for various items of regular purchase; |
| 4. | Liaison with State Purchase Cell and Customs; |
| 5. | Processing of write-off & disposal of unserviceable stores/equipment; |
| 6. | Advising in the framing of rules for Purchase and Maintenance of Stock Registers, Stock Verification, write-off, etc.; |
| 7. | Advising Departments, Centers for Technologies and other academic service facilities with respect to stores & purchase; and |
| 8. | Advertisements on request of Offices of the Institute. |
