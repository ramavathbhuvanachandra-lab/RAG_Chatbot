# Office of Research and Development — Research

## Scope

Authoritative institutional knowledge for Office of Research and Development — Research at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Welcome To The Office Of Research & Development (R&D)


The Office of R&D provides specialized administrative and managerial support for the operation of Sponsored Research Projects, Consultancy Projects and other R&D related activities of the Institute. The Office of R&D facilitates interaction with national and international agencies supporting research & development. Also, it promotes academia-industry interactions and collaboration with larger R&D laboratories, and liaises between Institute and funding agencies. Further, it supports Faculty Members in filing of patents and documenting IPR generated through research activity of the Institute. The Office of R&D facilitates the following:
**(a) Management of Projects**
1. Registration of Sponsored, Consulting and Institute Projects, raising Invoice/Grants-in-aid bill, receiving funds, prepare utilization certificates;
2. Appointment, extension and resignations of Project Staff Members; payment of salary;
3. Work related to Fellowships;
4. Project related purchases, imports and their payments;
5. Management of temporary advances to PIs and Project Staff Members, settlement of advances;
6. Payment for work assignments; and
7. Preparation of cheques, bank reconciliation, accounts preparation, management of investments.
**(b) Management of Office**
1. Preparation and publishing of booklets (hard and soft copies) related to Office of R&D, newsletter;
2. Institute’s research magazine;
3. Developing & maintaining database of R&D contacts in Industry, Academia and R&D Organisations in India and abroad;
4. Designing and preparing R&D flyers of different Departments and laboratories of the Institute; and
5. Managing MOUs, IPRs, Corporate Communications, Events and Relationships.
