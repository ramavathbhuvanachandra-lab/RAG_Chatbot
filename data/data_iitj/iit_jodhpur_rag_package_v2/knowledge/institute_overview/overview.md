# Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur — Overview

## Scope

Authoritative institutional knowledge for Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur — Overview at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Institutional Profile

Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur (IIT Jodhpur) was established in 2008 as one of the second-generation IITs by the Ministry of Education (formerly Ministry of Human Resource Development), Government of India. The Institute is situated on a sprawling 852-acre campus on National Highway 62 in Karwar, Jodhpur, Rajasthan.

## Academic and Research Vision

IIT Jodhpur is dedicated to nurturing excellence in engineering, science, humanities, and interdisciplinary domains. The Institute operates through evidenced academic departments, specialized interdisciplinary schools (such as Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, Management & Entrepreneurship, Design, and Liberal Arts), and multidisciplinary research platforms designed to solve technological and societal challenges of national and international significance.

## Residential and Campus Infrastructure

The Institute adheres to a fully residential model comprising 17 student hostels, state-of-the-art computational infrastructure, primary healthcare facilities, centralized sports amenities, and advanced fabrication and instrumentation laboratories.
