# Primary Health Centre (PHC) & Medical Services

## Scope

Authoritative institutional knowledge for Primary Health Centre (PHC) & Medical Services at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Healthcare Infrastructure & 24/7 Operations

The Health Centre (HC) at the Permanent Campus of IIT Jodhpur operates 24/7 to provide primary medical care, outpatient services, and emergency stabilization to students, faculty, staff members, registered dependents, and campus residents.

The Health Centre is supervised by the Professor-in-Charge Health Center (PIC-HC) and Institute Medical Officers, advised by the Medical Service User's Committee (MSUC).

## Clinical Facilities & In-House Services

The Health Centre offers comprehensive on-campus medical amenities:
1. **24x7 Medical Officers & Nursing Officers**: Rotating medical staff providing round-the-clock emergency and clinical coverage.
2. **In-House Pharmacy**: Dispensing essential prescription medications and emergency medicines directly on campus.
3. **Diagnostic Laboratory & Testing**: Pathology, routine diagnostics, and medical screening tests.
4. **Physiotherapy Unit**: Rehabilitation and musculoskeletal treatment.
5. **Emergency & Daycare Wards**: Dedicated 24x7 Emergency Room, Daycare Room, and separate male and female observation and isolation wards.
6. **Ambulance Services**: 01 Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) ambulance and 01 Basic Life Support (BLS) ambulance stationed on campus 24/7 for emergency hospital transit.
7. **Alternative Medicine Consultations**: Visiting Ayurvedic, Homeopathic, and Naturopathic consultants holding weekly clinics.

## Specialist Doctor Clinics

In addition to resident Medical Officers, specialist physicians conduct weekly outpatient clinics on campus:
- **Internal Medicine / MD Physician**: Available daily (except Sundays) from 3:30 PM to 7:00 PM.
- **Specialists**: Regular weekly visits by specialists in Psychiatry, Gynaecology & Obstetrics, Paediatrics, ENT, Orthopaedics, and Dental Services.

## Empanelled Hospital Network & Cashless IPD

For specialized tertiary care and secondary hospitalization, IIT Jodhpur maintains formal agreements with leading multi-specialty hospitals in Jodhpur:
1. **AIIMS Jodhpur**
2. **Goyal Hospital and Research Centre**
3. **MediPulse Hospital**
4. **ASG Eye Hospital**
5. **Vasundhara Hospital**

The Institute Health Centre coordinates admissions, treatment monitoring, and cashless IPD facilities for eligible students and employees.
