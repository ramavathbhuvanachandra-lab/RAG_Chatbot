# Visitor Hostel Accommodation and Booking Charges

## Scope

Authoritative charges, booking rules, and occupancy conditions for short-term and long-term visitor guest hostel accommodation at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Short-Term Hostel Accommodation Rates (01 – 10 Days)

The hostel provides basic furnished air-cooled rooms with beds, study furniture, and optional bedding:

### A. Rates for Visitors and Executive Students (Excluding GST):
* Double Occupancy (without bedding): ₹450/- per day per person.
* Single Occupancy (without bedding, subject to availability): ₹750/- per day per person.
* Double Occupancy (with 1 bedding set): ₹600/- per day per person.
* Single Occupancy (with 1 bedding set): ₹900/- per day per person.

### B. Rates for Students, Staff, and Faculty of IIT Jodhpur:
* Double Occupancy (without bedding): ₹300/- per day per person.
* Single Occupancy (without bedding): ₹500/- per day per person.
* Double Occupancy (with 1 bedding set): ₹400/- per day per person.
* Single Occupancy (with 1 bedding set): ₹600/- per day per person.
* Note: All rates include bucket and mug in attached bathroom suites.

## Long-Term Bookings (11 Days to 6 Months)

* For duration exceeding 11 days but less than a month: ₹2,175/- (excluding GST).
* For duration exceeding one month: ₹2,175/- per month (excluding GST).
* Applicability: Applicable to all external visitors (with GST) and IIT Jodhpur community members (without GST).
* Post-Thesis Ph.D. Policy: These monthly rates apply to Ph.D. scholars who wish to avail hostel accommodation for more than one month following thesis submission without regular semester fee payment.

## Booking Protocol

All visitor accommodation reservations must be submitted online and approved through the Office of Students / Hostel Administration portal.
