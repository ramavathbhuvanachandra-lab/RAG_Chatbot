# Academic Fees, Tuition Waivers, and Scholarships

## Scope

Authoritative institutional fee structures, tuition remission policies, and scholarship provisions across all undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral degree programmes at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Academic Fee Structures by Programme

### 1. B.Tech. and B.S. Programmes (AY 2026-2027)

Under Ministry of Education (MoE) national IIT norms, the tuition fee structure for regular undergraduate students is:
* Tuition Fee: ₹1,00,000/- per semester.
* SC, ST, and PwD Students: 100% Tuition Fee Exemption (Zero Tuition Fee).
* Economically Backward Students (Family income < ₹1,00,000 per annum): 100% Tuition Fee Remission.
* Economically Backward Students (Family income ₹1,00,000 to ₹5,00,000 per annum): Remission of 2/3rd of the tuition fee (Student pays ₹33,333/- per semester).
* Vidyalaxmi Scheme: Access to interest-free education loans for the entire payable tuition portion.

### 2. ITEP (4-Year B.Sc. B.Ed.) Programme (AY 2026-2027)

Under the approved fee structure for the Integrated Teacher Education Programme:
* Tuition Fee: ₹50,000/- per semester. (SC, ST, and PwD students are fully exempted).
* Admission Fee (One-Time): ₹3,800/-.
* Convocation Fee (One-Time): ₹4,000/-.
* Semester Fee (Includes Hostel Room Rent): ₹32,250/- per semester.
* Refundable Security Deposit (One-Time): ₹8,000/-.
* Experiential & Professional Development Fee: ₹2,000/- per semester.
* Mess Advance: Payable separately each semester based on dining consumption.

### 3. MBA and Tech MBA Programme (School of Management & Entrepreneurship)

Under the approved SME fee schedule for the 2026-2028 batch:
* Tuition Fee: ₹2,25,000/- per semester.
* Category Exemption: SC, ST, and PwD students are exempt from paying tuition fees as per Government of India regulations.
* International Candidates (SAARC): 1.5 times the Indian student tuition fee.
* International Candidates (Non-SAARC): 2.0 times the Indian student tuition fee.

## Scholarships and Financial Assistantships

* Teaching Assistantship (M.Tech.): ₹12,400/- per month for GATE-qualified scholars as per MoE norms.
* Ph.D. Research Fellowship: ₹37,000/- per month (Junior Research Fellow - JRF for Years 1–2) and ₹42,000/- per month (Senior Research Fellow - SRF for Years 3–5).
* Central Sector Scholarship Scheme: Full tuition fee waiver, living allowance, and book grant for meritorious SC/ST students.
* Merit-cum-Means (MCM) Scholarship: Full tuition waiver and pocket allowance for eligible undergraduate scholars.
