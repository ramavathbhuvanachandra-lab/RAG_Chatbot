# Office of Vocational Training and Upskilling (VTU)

## Scope

Authoritative mandate, national skill alignment, and historical programme archive of the Office of Vocational Training & Upskilling (VTU) at Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur.

## Mandate and Organizational Vision

The Office of Vocational Training & Upskilling (VTU) at IIT Jodhpur functions as a nodal institutional centre for short-term, vocational, skill-based, and executive upskilling initiatives.

Key objectives of the Office of VTU:
* Support national capacity-building initiatives under Skill India (PMKVY), Digital Governance (GeM/GFR), and Mission Karmayogi.
* Deliver quality-assured training using IIT Jodhpur's advanced academic, computing, and laboratory infrastructure.
* Provide structured vocational pathways alongside conventional university degree programmes.

## Strategic National Pillars

VTU initiatives align directly with three key national pillars:
* Skill India (PMKVY): Empowering youth and technical workforces with hands-on industrial skills.
* Digital and Financial Governance: Executive compliance training on Government e-Marketplace (GeM) and General Financial Rules (GFR 2017).
* Mission Karmayogi: Behavioral, ethical, and leadership capacity building for public administration.

## Archive of Completed Training Programmes (2023–2024)

> Historical Archive Notice: The training schedules listed below represent completed past programmes. Registration is closed. For upcoming training schedules, consult the Office of VTU.

### 1. Specialized Training in Government e-Marketplace (GeM)
* Programme Status: Completed (Conducted 17–18 June 2023).
* Sector: Digital Public Procurement & Governance.
* Audience: Government and Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) procurement officials.
* Mode and Duration: Hybrid delivery over two intensive days.
* Covered Modules: Buyer & Seller registration, bid formulation, Reverse Auctions, Consignee Receipt and Acceptance Certificate (CRAC), and online payment workflows.

### 2. Specialized Training on General Financial Rules (GFR 2017)
* Programme Status: Completed (Conducted 10–11 June 2023).
* Sector: Public Financial Management and Statutory Compliance.
* Audience: Government administrative officers, finance executives, and procurement heads.
* Covered Modules: GFR 2017 compliance, procurement of goods and consulting services, Department of Expenditure (DoE) guidelines.

### 3. Large-Scale Skilling Hub (PMKVY 4.0)
* Programme Status: Phase-I Completed (November 2023 – June 2024).
* Scope: Comprehensive vocational skilling conducted at the IIT Jodhpur Skill Hub under the Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana.
